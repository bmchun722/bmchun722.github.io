About

I am a master's student in the Stefan Ringe Group at Korea University, focused on multiscale modeling, numerical analysis, and optimization for electrochemical systems. My research centers on developing reliable computational tools that enable efficient kinetic analysis and mechanistic understanding of CO₂ reduction reactions — bridging atomistic simulations and continuum-level descriptions.
